# Amazon-Sales-Report
Amazon Sales Dashboard – Power BI Analytics Project

This dashboard was created to analyze Amazon sales performance using interactive visualizations and business intelligence techniques. The dashboard helps track sales trends, courier status, product demand, fulfillment performance, and state-wise shipment analysis.

1. Business Problem

Businesses often struggle to analyze large amounts of sales data and identify important patterns from shipped, cancelled, and unshipped orders. Without proper visualization, it becomes difficult to:

Compare yearly sales performance
Monitor courier and fulfillment status
Identify top-selling products
Track shipment performance across different states
Improve delivery and operational efficiency

This dashboard solves these problems by converting raw SQL and Excel data into meaningful business insights.

2. Goal of the Dashboard

The main goal of this dashboard is to help businesses make data-driven decisions by providing a clear overview of Amazon sales operations.

Objectives:
Analyze overall sales amount and order trends
Compare shipped, cancelled, and unshipped orders
Identify high-demand product categories
Monitor courier and fulfillment performance
Track state-wise shipment distribution
Improve customer satisfaction and delivery efficiency

Using this dashboard, management can quickly identify weak areas and improve business performance.

3. Walkthrough of Key Visuals
Sales Amount KPI

Displays the total sales amount generated from Amazon sales data.

Date Filter/Slicer

Allows users to filter and compare sales performance between different dates and periods.

Courier Status Chart

The clustered column chart shows the count of courier statuses such as:

Shipped
On the Way
Cancelled
Unshipped

This helps track delivery performance and cancelled orders.

Product Category Analysis

The clustered bar chart shows which product categories customers ordered the most, such as:

T-shirts
Shirts
Blazers
Wallets
Perfumes

This helps identify high-demand products.

Courier Status Donut Chart

Displays percentage distribution of shipped, cancelled, and unshipped orders for better operational analysis.

Fulfillment Analysis

The fulfillment charts compare:

Ekart vs Easy Ship
Amazon vs Merchant fulfillment

This helps understand which fulfillment partner performs better.

State-wise Shipment Analysis

The line/waterfall chart shows shipment counts across different states, helping identify regions with higher order volumes.

4. Key Insights from the Dashboard
Most orders were successfully shipped, indicating strong delivery performance.
Cancelled and unshipped orders are comparatively lower but still require monitoring.
T-shirts and shirts are the most ordered product categories.
Amazon fulfillment handled more orders compared to merchant fulfillment.
Certain states contributed significantly higher shipment counts, showing strong regional demand.
Courier performance data helps identify logistics efficiency and operational gaps.

6. Business Impact

This dashboard helps businesses improve strategy and operational efficiency in several ways:

Business Benefits:
Improves decision-making using real-time visual insights
Helps reduce cancelled and delayed orders
Identifies top-performing products and regions
Enhances supply chain and courier management
Supports better inventory planning
Helps management track KPIs quickly and effectively
Improves customer satisfaction through delivery monitoring

By using this dashboard, businesses can optimize sales operations, improve logistics performance, and increase profitability.
